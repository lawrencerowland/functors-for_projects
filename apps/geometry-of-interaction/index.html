The project

Fit out one island reading room. Here, we model only the exchange that proposes a delivery plan.

The question

What happens when one coordinator’s answer becomes the other coordinator’s next input?

The mathematical move

Connect two signed interfaces, follow their feedback, then hide the internal conversation.

    03 / Hide the conversation

    What can the outside world see?

    Composition follows the internal exchanges until a message exits. Hiding removes those middle messages from the resulting partial function.

    g ∘ f : A⁺ ⊔ C⁻ ⇀ A⁻ ⊔ C⁺

    One request produces a survey question. A separately supplied report may produce a proposed plan. These are two externally started episodes, not an automatically completed conversation.

    Exterior inputExterior output of g ∘ f

    Computed from the current rules. “Undefined” means there is no entry in the partial function; the diagnostic distinguishes a missing rule from a witnessed cycle.

    An identity copies each signed message through. Composition can be grouped either way. The model’s automated checks exercise identity and associativity on finite examples; those checks are not a general mathematical proof.

    04 / Source → concept → construction → limit

    The idea behind the island.

    01 / Source

    Symmetric feedback

    The starting image is Abramsky’s two-box Geometry of Interaction diagram: a shared interface carries traffic in both directions.

    Abramsky’s slide, p. 9 ↗
    02 / Concept

    Interaction & hiding

    A’s and C’s signed ports stay outside. The B ports connect the components, and composition follows their interaction before hiding B.

    GoI tutorial, p. 32 ↗
    03 / Construction

    A finite routing model

    Each box is a typed partial function. The executable construction illustrates finite Int(Pfn, ⊔): disjoint-union interfaces with explicit missing routes and exact-state cycle detection.

    GoI construction, pp. 28–33 ↗
    04 / Limit

    A plan, not a voyage

    These are authored routing policies, not full game strategies. Legal plays, histories, payoffs and strategic incentives need more structure. No physical delivery, safe passage or human benefit is established here.
